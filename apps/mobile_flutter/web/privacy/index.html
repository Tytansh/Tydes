Privacy Policy

Tydes helps surfers discover spots, view forecast information, plan trips, find stays and connect with the surf community. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information such as email address, display name, handle, profile details and verification status.
  • Content you create, including posts, events, comments, messages, media uploads, reports, blocks, favorites and alerts.
  • Surf preferences such as saved spots, selected forecast spot, premium status, notification preferences and language preference.
  • Purchase and subscription information from Apple, Google, Stripe/Link and RevenueCat, including the purchased plan, transaction identifiers, payment status and renewal or expiry dates. Payment details are collected by the payment provider; Tydes does not receive or store full payment card numbers.
  • Basic technical data such as device type, app version, request logs, crash information, IP address and approximate region needed to run and secure the service.

How we use information

  • To create and secure accounts, send verification or reset emails and prevent duplicate handles.
  • To show forecasts, tides, alerts, maps, posts, events, messages, profiles, favorites and premium features.
  • To personalize spot and travel discovery and show relevant accommodation options by surf area.
  • To moderate content, process reports, block users, prevent spam and keep the community safer.
  • To improve Tydes, diagnose bugs, protect against abuse and provide customer support.

Optional Google sign-in

The sign-in page uses Google Identity Services to display a Google sign-in option. If you choose it, we use your Google account's email, email-verification status and stable account identifier to create or find your Tydes account. We verify Google's sign-in token on our server and do not store that token. We do not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail or Google Drive. Google may process browser and device information when its sign-in service loads, under Google's Privacy Policy. Deleting your Tydes account removes its Google account link; it does not delete your Google account.

Forecasts, tides and location data

Tydes uses surf spots, map coordinates, forecast providers, tide providers and cached forecast data to show surf-planning information. Forecast and tide information is for planning only and is not a navigation or safety-critical service.

Accommodation and affiliate links

When you open an accommodation or booking link, you leave Tydes and visit a third-party service. That service may receive standard referral information and handle your activity under its own privacy policy. Tydes may receive a commission from qualifying bookings without increasing the price you pay.

Sharing information

We share information with service providers that help operate Tydes, such as hosting, database, media storage, email delivery, forecast and tide data, payment entitlement, analytics, app-store and accommodation referral services. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users, Tydes or the public.

For website subscriptions, Stripe and, where available, Link process checkout, billing and subscription management. RevenueCat verifies purchases and Premium access. We share your Tydes account identifier with RevenueCat to associate a purchase with your account. Contact information you enter during checkout, payment details and transaction information are handled by the payment providers under their privacy policies. Tydes receives subscription status and billing identifiers needed to provide access and support. See Stripe's Privacy Policy, Link's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile information inside the app.
  • You can delete your account from app settings.
  • You can block users and report content from social surfaces.
  • You can manage app-store subscriptions through Apple or Google account settings. For website subscriptions, use Manage subscription in Tydes Premium settings to open the billing provider's portal.
  • You can contact us for help accessing, correcting or deleting data.

Children

Tydes is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.
