TYDES support

How can we help?

Contact us for account help, premium purchase issues, forecast problems, content reports, safety concerns, accommodation partnerships or data deletion questions.

Email TYDESsupport@tydes.io

Include your account email, handle, device type and what happened so we can help faster. Never send your password.

Account helpLogin, verification, password reset or account deletion questions. Premium helpSubscription status, purchase restoration or feature access. Forecast issueReport a spot, forecast, tide, map or alert problem. PartnershipsAccommodation, affiliate, surf business or destination collaboration.

Account deletion

You can delete your account inside the app from Profile Settings. If you cannot access your account, email support from the address linked to it.

Premium and Restore Purchases

App-store purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Website subscriptions use Stripe and, where available, Link Managed Payments. Open Premium in TYDES settings and choose Manage subscription for website billing or cancellation. Use Restore purchases or Check subscription on the Premium screen if a paid subscription is not showing on the TYDES account used for the purchase. Never send us your card number or password.

Reports and blocks

Use the report or block options on posts, comments, profiles and messages if you see harassment, scams, sexual services, nudity, hate, spam or dangerous event promotion.

Surf safety

TYDES forecast and tide information is for planning only. Always check local conditions, tide, hazards, weather and advice before paddling out.
