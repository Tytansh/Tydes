About TYDES

Where We Surfing

A surf community for wherever the next session takes you.

TYDES brings surf spots, forecast tools and people together. Find a break, get some local context, and see who’s heading out—whether you’re close to home or exploring a new coast.

Take a look around → The public map is open. No account needed.

In TYDES today

Find your break.
Meet your crew.

For surfers finding their next local session, meeting a crew or travelling between surf regions.

  1. 01

    Explore the breaks

    Browse the public surf map, spot guides and upcoming public events. Filter by country or region, from Australia and Taiwan to Sri Lanka and Southeast Asia.

  2. 02

    Plan your session

    Use the forecast, tide and alert tools available to your account. Save favourite spots and keep your surf planning in one place.

  3. 03

    Find your people

    Create a profile, share photos and clips, join surf meetups and chat with the people going. An account is needed for community features.

We’re building a more useful connection between choosing a break and planning the rest of the trip.

Accommodation discovery, scooter rentals, board shops and personal trip-planning tools are planned. These features are not yet available, and stays cannot currently be booked through TYDES.

The accommodation experience we’re working towards

  1. 1
    Pick a surf area

    Start with the coast and breaks you want to explore.

  2. 2
    Discover relevant stays

    See accommodation from an approved future partner.

  3. 3
    Book with the provider

    Continue to their official site for prices, availability and checkout.

We welcome conversations with hostel operators, accommodation platforms and local surf businesses. Future integrations would depend on agreed partner access; booking terms, payments, cancellations and booking support would remain with the provider.
