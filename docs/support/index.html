Tydes support

How can we help?

Contact Tydes support for account help, premium purchase issues, forecast problems, content reports, safety concerns, or data deletion questions.

Email support support@tydes.io Include your account email, handle, device type, and what happened so we can help faster.

Account deletion

You can delete your account inside the app from Profile Settings. If you cannot access your account, email support from the email address linked to the account.

Premium and restore purchases

Premium purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Use Restore Purchases in the Tydes premium screen if your subscription is active but the app does not show premium access.

Reports and blocks

Use the report or block options on posts, profiles, and messages if you see harassment, scams, sexual services, nudity, hate, spam, or dangerous event promotion.

Surf safety

Tydes forecast and tide information is for planning only. Always check local conditions, tide, hazards, weather, and advice before paddling out.
