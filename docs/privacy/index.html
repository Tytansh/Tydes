Privacy Policy

Tydes helps surfers discover spots, view forecast information, plan trips, and connect with the surf community. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information such as email address, display name, handle, profile details, and verification status.
  • Content you create, including posts, events, comments, messages, media uploads, reports, blocks, favorites, and alerts.
  • Surf app preferences such as saved spots, selected free live forecast spot, premium status, notification preferences, and language preference.
  • Purchase and subscription information from Apple, Google, Stripe/Link, and RevenueCat, including the purchased plan, transaction identifiers, payment status, and renewal or expiry dates. Payment details are collected by the payment provider; Tydes does not receive or store full payment card numbers.
  • Basic technical data such as device type, app version, request logs, crash/debug information, IP address, and approximate region needed to run and secure the service.

How we use information

  • To create and secure accounts, send verification or reset emails, and prevent duplicate handles.
  • To show forecasts, tides, alerts, maps, posts, events, messages, profiles, favorites, and premium features.
  • To moderate content, process reports, block users, prevent spam, and keep the community safer.
  • To improve Tydes, diagnose bugs, protect against abuse, and understand which features need work.
  • To provide customer support and respond to legal, safety, or security requests.

Forecasts, tides, and location data

Tydes uses surf spots, map coordinates, forecast providers, tide providers, and cached forecast data to show surf-planning information. Forecast and tide information is for planning only and is not a navigation or safety-critical service.

Sharing information

We share information with service providers that help operate Tydes, such as hosting, database, media storage, email delivery, forecast and tide data, payment entitlement, and app-store services. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users, Tydes, or the public.

For website subscriptions, Stripe and, where available, Link process checkout, billing, and subscription management. RevenueCat verifies purchases and Premium access. We share your Tydes account identifier with RevenueCat to associate a purchase with your account. Contact information you enter during checkout, payment details, and transaction information are handled by the payment providers under their privacy policies. Tydes receives subscription status and billing identifiers needed to provide access and support. See Stripe's Privacy Policy, Link's Privacy Policy, and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile information inside the app.
  • You can delete your account from the app settings.
  • You can block users and report content from social surfaces.
  • You can manage app-store subscriptions through Apple or Google account settings. For website subscriptions, use Manage subscription in Tydes Premium settings to open the billing provider's portal.
  • You can contact us if you need help accessing, correcting, or deleting data.

Children

Tydes is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.
